Capture it. Pin it. Read it.

Snapixer is a fast screenshot tool for Windows. Press F1, select an area, and copy, pin, annotate, recognize text or ask an AI about it, right next to your selection.

Get it from the Microsoft Store

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit)

What it does

Capture in one keystroke

F1 freezes the screen. Drag to select an area, or click a window to capture it exactly along its frame. Works across several monitors and mixed display scaling.

Pin on top

Keep any screenshot floating above all windows while you work. Move it, zoom it, make it translucent, and pin as many as you need.

Offline text recognition

Select and copy text straight from the screenshot. English is built in, and more than 90 other languages can be downloaded once and then work offline.

Annotate and present

Arrows, shapes, pen, highlighter, text, pixelate and blur. Smart trim and polished frames turn a capture into a slide or a social media image.

QR codes and web search

Read QR codes offline and decide yourself whether to open a link. Search the web for any area with Google Lens or Bing.

Optional AI assistant

Ask about what you selected: explain, solve, translate, extract text. Bring your own key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter, or run a local Ollama.

A second chance

Missed the right area? Make a new selection on the same frozen screen, or browse earlier screenshots with the arrow keys.

Your language, your keys

Interface in 30 languages. Keyboard shortcuts can be changed and work with any keyboard layout.

Private by default

  • No account, no ads, no usage statistics or crash reports sent to us.
  • Screenshots, recognized text and settings stay on your computer.
  • Data leaves your computer only when you use the AI assistant (to the provider you choose) or web search (to Google or Microsoft through your browser).
