Snapixer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Snapixer is a screenshot, pin and text recognition (OCR) app for Windows, published by Asen Tolsuzov ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: Snapixer has no account, shows no ads and does not collect usage statistics, analytics or crash reports. Your screenshots stay on your computer unless you use one of the optional online features listed below.

Data stored on your computer

Snapixer keeps the following data in your Windows user profile. We have no access to any of it.

  • Screenshot history: the images you capture, their thumbnails, and any drawings you add.
  • Kept full screens (for "New selection"): a copy of everything that was on all monitors when a screenshot was taken. They are deleted after 24 hours by default; you can shorten this, turn it off, or delete them at any time in Settings. They are not encrypted.
  • Settings, including your chosen languages and shortcuts.
  • OCR language files you download.
  • AI provider API keys, if you enter any. They are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) for your Windows account and are never shown again or sent anywhere other than to the provider they belong to.
  • A diagnostic log file that records errors so you can send it with a support request if you choose. It stays on your computer; key-like strings are masked.

Text recognition (OCR), QR code reading and the detection of window positions under the cursor run entirely on your computer. Window positions are only used while you select an area and are not stored.

You can delete the history and kept screens in the app. Uninstalling Snapixer from the Microsoft Store removes all of the data above.

Data sent over the internet

Data leaves your computer only when you use one of these optional features:

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom
AI chatThe selected screenshot (with your drawings) and the messages you type. The AI provider you configure yourself (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, or your own server), using your own API key.
Search by imageThe selected screenshot (with your drawings). Your web browser uploads it. Google Lens or Microsoft Bing, whichever you choose in Settings.
Download an OCR languageOnly the request for the language file; no screenshots or text. The public content delivery networks jsDelivr or unpkg.

Those services receive your IP address like any website and handle the data under their own privacy policies, which we do not control. We never receive a copy of what you send to them. When you open a link from a QR code, it opens in your default web browser.

Purchases, licensing and updates are handled by the Microsoft Store under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking or crash reporting.
  • We do not run any servers that receive your screenshots or text.

Children

Snapixer is a general purpose productivity tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact on the support page, which is also listed on the Snapixer page in the Microsoft Store.
